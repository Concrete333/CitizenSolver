How these priorities become a ranking

Each stat is converted to a 0–100 preference score using a fixed diminishing-return curve, then multiplied by your percentage. The result is a preference match, not a combat win rate. Zero means “ignore this stat.” Builds missing a stat you prioritize are excluded.

Stealth combines EM, IR and hull cross-section, averaging the strongest signal across the three exported axes. Handling combines rated pitch/yaw (70%), braking (20%) and forward acceleration (10%); power-dependent flight is not simulated. Hull is the sum of exported part HP, and armor is the exported armor-health rating — neither is effective hit points or penetration resistance. Cargo counts grid SCU, not mining bags.

Half-score reference points: 1,500 engagement DPS; 10,000 shield HP; 10,000 signature; handling components: 60°/s turning, 50 m/s² braking, 100 m/s² acceleration; 1,000 m/s speed; 20,000 hull HP; 10,000 armor HP; 100 SCU. These initial curves are tunable product assumptions.
